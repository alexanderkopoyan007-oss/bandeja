# Strokes measured on video

Pose read in the browser from public clips (MediaPipe pose landmarker, heavy model, 1.2.0), a frame at a time, on the clip's own
page. Nothing but these numbers was kept: no frame, no landmark file, no clip. Measures: hips and stance in leg lengths, elbow as
flexion (0 straight), wrist height in trunk lengths above (+) or below (-) the shoulders.

Limits. Slowed clips dilate time, so times below are scaled by estimate. A coach's demonstration has a higher, closer ball than a
match. Depth from one camera is poor, so stance width reads narrow and is only compared within a clip.

Checked for mistake demonstrations after the serve was found copied from one (2026-10-31): clip 1 is a pros' reel; clips 2 and 3 are
wordless slow motion of pro strokes; clip 4's window is correct demonstrations (transcript read); clip 6's forehand windows were re-read
with the transcript. Clip 5 (`jRkQB25IGPw`) and clip 7 (`5MH8RDf4qfg`) and `YawHc7Cv8us` have no captions: clip 7 shows no overlay at 30 s
or 37 s, but what its coach says in that window is unknown, so the backhand rows are not proven to be the right way.

A warning about these clips, learned the hard way. Until 31 Oct the serve here was measured at 32.9-37.7 s of clip 7, which is the
coach's example of a club player just getting the rally started, and the bent-over serve around 33-34 s carries red crosses on
screen. That moved the game's contact down to 0.70 m and bent the trunk to 33-41 degrees: the owner's "the serve motion is wrong".
Read the coach's words before measuring a clip, and never trust a window nobody looked at. Clip 6's strokes after 2:05 are backhands.
